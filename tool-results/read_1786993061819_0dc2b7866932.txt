     4→Product Requirements Document + Master Development Prompt
     5→Version: 1.0

    10→---
    11→1. Product Vision
    12→DARKO is a browser-first virtual hangout platform where people can create a room and instantly invite friends to watch
    13→content together, talk through voice/video, share screens, chat, exchange files/links, play multiplayer games, browse
    14→together, and customize their shared space.
    15→The product must feel like a premium social entertainment operating system rather than a basic watch-party page.
    16→Core principle:
    17→Create a room, share one link, and everything needed for a digital hangout is inside the room.
    18→Guests should be able to join without creating an account. Google authentication is available for users who want
    19→persistent profiles, saved rooms, preferences, history, or ownership.
    20→---
    21→2. Research-Based Feature Baseline
    22→Current Kosmi materials describe a browser-based entertainment/hangout product with rooms, synchronized video,
    23→games, shared browsing, screen sharing, link/file sharing, voice/video/messaging, customizable rooms, and public/private
    24→room concepts. Kosmi also promotes no-download access and free usage.
    25→DARKO should reproduce the underlying product category and workflows while using:
    26→(cid:127) original branding
    27→(cid:127) original UI
    28→(cid:127) original copy
    29→(cid:127) original assets
    30→(cid:127) original architecture
    31→(cid:127) open-source/free technologies where possible
    32→Do not scrape, proxy, or redistribute copyrighted streaming content.
